# docs/brd/

This directory contains Business Requirements Documents (BRDs) for Atlas.

## Purpose

A BRD defines the business context that drives a product initiative:

- Business objectives and stakeholder needs
- Regulatory and compliance constraints
- Commercial and contractual requirements
- Business rules that the system must enforce
- ROI expectations and business metrics

## Naming Convention

```
BRD-<id>-<short-title>.md
```

Example: `BRD-001-billing-compliance.md`

## Documents

> BRDs are added as business initiatives are defined. Nothing is documented here yet.
