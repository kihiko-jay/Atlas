# docs/vision/

This directory contains the Atlas product vision — the foundational "why" behind everything we build.

## Purpose

Vision documents articulate:

- The problem Atlas exists to solve
- The world we are trying to create for our users
- The strategic bets and principles that guide product direction
- What success looks like at 1 year, 3 years, and 5 years

## Documents

> Vision documents are added as the product strategy is defined. Nothing is documented here yet.

## Governance

Vision documents are owned by product leadership and reviewed quarterly. Engineering decisions that conflict with the vision require explicit escalation.
